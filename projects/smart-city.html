PROJECT 02 / SMART CITY

Cooling Mist System

Interactive 3D Smart City Prototype

A Smart City cooling mist simulation prototype exploring the use of technology and interactive 3D visualization to present an urban cooling concept.

ROLE

Technology Leader

PROGRAM

World Friends Korea ICT Volunteer Program

TECHNOLOGIES

Three.js

01 / OVERVIEW

About the Project

Cooling Mist System was developed as part of a Smart City concept during the World Friends Korea ICT Volunteer Program. The project explored an urban cooling solution through a prototype that combined physical scale models and interactive 3D visualization.

The prototype aimed to present the concept in a more tangible and engaging way, allowing ideas about technology and urban environments to be communicated visually.

02 / CONTRIBUTIONS

My Role & Contributions

As the Technology Leader, I was responsible for contributing to the technical direction of the prototype and collaborating with team members throughout its development.

Technical Leadership

Helped guide the technical development of the prototype and coordinated technical work within the team.

3D Visualization

Developed interactive 3D visualizations using Three.js to represent the cooling mist concept.

International Collaboration

Collaborated with South Korean university students in developing the Smart City concept and its physical scale models.

03 / TECHNOLOGIES

Tools & Technologies

Three.js

04 / REFLECTION

What I Learned

Participating in the World Friends Korea ICT Volunteer Program gave me an opportunity to explore how technology can contribute to real-world environmental challenges. As the Technology Leader, I learned to take responsibility for technical decisions while working collaboratively with an international team.

Developing an interactive 3D visualization using Three.js challenged me to think beyond conventional web development and explore new ways of presenting technological concepts.

This experience taught me that innovation is not only about creating something technically impressive, but also about understanding the problem, communicating ideas, and working together to turn concepts into tangible prototypes.
